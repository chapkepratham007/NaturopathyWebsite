About Naturopathy & Yoga Diploma

MSBVE Recognized 1-Year Professional Certification | Course Code: 201208

Our Vision: Disease-Free Living Through Natural Cure

Manohar Vasudha Foundation was established with the noble objective of creating skilled naturopaths and yoga therapists. Led by experienced Ayurvedic doctors and wellness experts, the foundation bridges ancient Indian Panchmahabhuta healing with modern clinical diagnostics.

Our 1-year diploma is officially recognized by the Maharashtra State Board of Vocational Education Examination (MSBVE), Mumbai. The curriculum emphasizes 6 hours of weekly theory alongside 18 hours of intensive practical lab training.
